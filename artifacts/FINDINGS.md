## 1. Material claim defect — fixed

The site publishes a blog post titled **"Stop saying 'HIPAA certified'"** and an
About page stating *"We also do not claim certifications we cannot evidence"*
(`components/sections/about/HowWeWorkSection.tsx:128`), while three other places
claimed exactly that kind of certification. `app/layout.tsx:121` already carried a
comment recording that "certified" had been removed there for this reason — these
three were missed by that pass.

| File | Was | Now |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `components/sections/solutions/PaySection.tsx:12` | "Mastercard and Visa **certified** … handling **billions** in annual transaction volume with **99.99% uptime SLA**" | "engineered to PCI-DSS, and taken through Mastercard and Visa scheme certification with the institutions that operate it" |
| `content/siteData.ts:8` | "**Certified** direct integrations with Mastercard and Visa networks" | "Direct integrations with Mastercard and Visa networks" |
| `content/siteData.ts:95` | "Mastercard & Visa **certified** … **$2.4B** annual transactions, **99.99%** uptime, **<200ms** authorization" | "Mastercard and Visa payment processing, engineered to PCI-DSS and taken through scheme certification" + `TODO(client)` for the figures |

The replacement wording is not invented — it matches the answer the site already
gives in its own `/services` FAQ: *"We have taken payment platforms through scheme
certification."* Scheme certification is issued to the institution operating the
platform, not to the development firm.

**Why this mattered.** For a vendor selling AML/transaction monitoring to
SBP-regulated banks, an unevidenced certification claim is misrepresentation
exposure, not a copy nit — and it directly contradicted the site's own published
policy on the same domain.

Verified: `grep -rniE "\bcertified\b"` now returns only the educational and FAQ
uses, which are correct. Build passes, no SEO regression.

## 5. Keyword demand — the strategic finding

Google Suggest, `--gl pk`. Prominence, not volume; there is no volume source on
this machine (Ahrefs, Semrush and DataForSEO all return plan/quota errors).

| Seed | Variants found |
| --- | --- |
| `software house islamabad` | **109** |
| `core banking software pakistan` | 4 |
| `aml software pakistan` | **0** |

The site is optimised hardest for AML/CFT and core-banking terminology, which
returns **almost no measurable Pakistani search demand**. The demand that exists
is for "software house islamabad".

Two caveats before acting on that:

1. Most of the 109 is **recruitment** intent — "software house in islamabad jobs"
   (20x), "for internship", "jobs for freshers". Not buyers.
2. The genuinely commercial slice is small but real: "top 10 software houses in
   islamabad" (11x), "software house in islamabad near me" (11x), "famous software
   houses in islamabad" (21x), "best software house in islamabad".

**The actionable link:** several high-frequency variants are *area-level* —
"blue area" (9x), "i8" (7x), "i 10" (4x). That makes CLIENT-ACTIONS item 5 (street
address) not schema hygiene but the unlock for demand that measurably exists. A
published Islamabad address plus a `/contact` page targeting "software house in
islamabad near me" is the highest-ratio move available right now.

This does **not** mean abandoning the AML positioning — that is a
high-value, low-volume enterprise sale where demand does not show up in
autocomplete. It means the local-discovery layer is currently unbuilt.

## What is automated now

```bash
node ruflo-office/pipeline.js
```

Runs build → claim verification → SEO/AEO/GEO audit → keyword refresh → report,
writes `artifacts/PIPELINE-REPORT.md`, and marks the office desks whose task it
actually completed. Exits non-zero on failure, so it is CI-safe.

Flags: `--no-build`, `--no-keywords`, `--no-office`.
